    LOCAL · PEER TO PEER

    Share live design

    Your folder stays on your device. Session setup is shared out of band; Tiny Image Star has no signaling server.

    Send both the design invitation and this short-lived session offer to your guest. Anyone with them can propose edits; your device validates and saves canonical changes. While connected, use the floating page and canvas controls to guide the guest; owner editing stays paused so local changes cannot drift from the shared copy. The channel you use can read both items, including network details in the offer, so share only through a channel you trust.

    Preparing a direct connection…

    Paste the invitation and current offer from the design owner. You need a writable folder because edits are kept as a local copy and become a local fork if the connection ends. Once connected, you follow the owner’s page and canvas view by default; pause following from the floating bar whenever you want to inspect another area. The service carrying your answer can read its network details, so use a channel you trust.
